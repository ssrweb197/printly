HELP & ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

Are Printly templates free?

The eight templates in our launch collection are currently offered as free PDF downloads.

How do I download a template?

Visit the template collection, choose a template and select its download link. Your browser may open or download the PDF.

How do I print a PDF?

Open the downloaded PDF in a PDF reader, select Print and check your paper size and print settings. Printer results may vary.

Can I use templates on my phone?

Most phones can open PDFs. Some templates are designed to be printed and filled in by hand.

Do I need an account?

No account is currently required to access the free PDF download links.

Can I resell or redistribute files?

Please do not resell, re-upload or redistribute the files as standalone products without written permission.
