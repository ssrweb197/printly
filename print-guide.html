Portrait

Choose portrait when the page is taller than it is wide, such as most planner and worksheet pages.

Landscape

Choose landscape when the page is wider than it is tall, such as some calendars and tracking sheets.

Scale: Fit to page

Use Fit or Shrink to printable area if content is close to the paper edges. This can reduce the printed size slightly.

Scale: Actual size

Use Actual size when the PDF is designed for the selected paper size and you want its intended dimensions.

BEFORE YOU PRINT

Quick print checklist

  • Confirm the paper size matches the PDF page dimensions.
  • Check the print preview for clipped text or missing page edges.
  • Choose colour or grayscale based on the design and your printer.
  • For double-sided printing, check the page order and flip setting first.
  • Print one test page before printing a large batch.
