What a monthly budget planner can help you track

A monthly budget sheet gives you one place to note expected income, regular bills, everyday spending and savings goals. Reviewing the page during the month can help you notice categories that need attention. The template is an organisational aid; it does not provide financial advice.

Preview before downloading

Open the Monthly Money Map page to view the PDF preview. Check that the layout suits the way you prefer to record your money before downloading or printing it.

A simple monthly routine

At the start of the month, write down expected income and fixed costs. Set realistic savings intentions, record variable spending as you go, and review the totals at month-end. Adjust your plan for the next month based on what you learned.

Print at home or a local print shop

The PDF can be viewed in a browser and printed using the paper-size and scaling options in your print dialog. Check the print preview before using paper.
